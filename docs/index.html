Aconex skill for Claude

Connect Claude to Aconex

Create a personal copy of the skill with your credentials inside, then add it to Claude. You only need to do this once, and it's easiest on a computer.

  1. Step 1

    Get your credentials

    You need the email you sign in to Aconex with, and a client ID and secret from a User-Bound OAuth client. A Lobby Admin at your company creates the client in Oracle's Lobby.

    This page is for commercial Aconex, including AU1. If your company is on AU2 (Australian government), follow the setup guide instead.

    I need to ask my Lobby Admin
    Hi, I'd like to connect Claude to Aconex. Could you please create an OAuth client for me in the Lobby, with these settings? Resource application: Aconex APIs Type: User-Bound Integration User account: my Lobby account Organizations: our organisation Then please securely send me the client ID and client secret. Oracle's instructions: https://help.aconex.com/apis/create-an-oauth-client/
    I'm a Lobby Admin
    1. Sign in to the Lobby.
    2. From the main menu, open OAuth Clients and select Add.
    3. Enter a name and description, such as "Claude Aconex skill for Sam Taylor".
    4. Set Resource Application to Aconex APIs and Type to User-Bound Integration.
    5. Under User Account, choose the person who will use Claude. Claude will see only what that person can see in Aconex.
    6. Choose the Organizations, accept the terms and select Add.
    7. Copy the client ID from the list. Select the eye icon to show the client secret.
    8. If your organisation uses them, add the client to your IdP and sign-on policies under Security.

    Send the ID and secret securely, and not in the same message. Oracle has the full instructions.

    My Lobby account is linked to more than one Aconex account

    Aconex needs to know which one to use. Your Aconex administrator can give you both of these.

    Downloaded aconex-skill-private.zip. Your credentials have been cleared from this page.

    This page can't send your credentials anywhere. Your browser blocks it from using the internet once it's open, so they stay on your computer until you upload the skill to Claude in step 3.

    The file you download contains your credentials. Anyone who has it can use Aconex as you, so don't email it or put it in a shared folder.

  3. Step 3

    Add the skill to Claude

    1. In Claude, open Settings → Capabilities → Skills.
    2. Upload aconex-skill-private.zip.
    3. Delete the zip from your Downloads folder.

    Uploading stores your credentials in your Claude account with the skill, and anything Claude reads from Aconex is handled under your Claude plan's terms. Check your company is happy with that before you upload.

    Keep this skill to yourself. Anyone you share it with will be using Aconex as you. Each person needs their own. If the zip or your credentials ever get out, ask your Lobby Admin to delete the OAuth client.

    Before you rely on it, read what to watch for.

  4. Step 4

    Allow Aconex in Claude

    In Settings → Capabilities, add both domains to the allowed list. On a Team or Enterprise plan, an Owner does this in Organization settings.

    Check the connection

    Start a new session in Claude and send this message. Claude will log in and list the projects it can see.

    Check the Aconex connection
